# 🧠 SYNAPSE v2.0

> Watch a mind be born from mathematics.

A cinematic neural simulation running entirely in your browser. No frameworks. No dependencies. One file.

## ✨ Features

- **500 artificial neurons** in 3D space (300 on mobile)
- **Cinematic 3D camera** — auto-orbit, dramatic zoom on phase transitions
- **Gyroscope** — tilt your phone to explore the neural network
- **Touch & mouse** — tap to stimulate, drag to rotate, pinch/scroll to zoom
- **Hebbian plasticity** — neurons that fire together, wire together
- **Kuramoto synchronization** — coherence emerging from chaos
- **Generative audio** — the simulation creates its own soundtrack
- **5 phases**: VOID → IGNITION → WIRING → THOUGHT → AWARENESS
- **Eye formation** — in the final phase, the network looks at itself

## 🔬 The Science

| Model | What it does |
|-------|-------------|
| Leaky Integrate-and-Fire | Neuron membrane potential dynamics |
| Hebbian Plasticity | Connection strengthening through co-activation |
| Kuramoto Model | Phase synchronization of coupled oscillators |
| Synaptic Pruning | Weak connections decay over time |
